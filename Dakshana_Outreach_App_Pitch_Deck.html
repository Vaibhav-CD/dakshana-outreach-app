The Solution

The Dakshana Outreach Portal at a Glance

A clean, 8-module single destination built for speed, mobile phones, and simple adoption.

1. Dashboard

Metrics, progress bars & Podium spotlights.

2. Notices

Broadcasts with batch filters & audio chimes.

3. Posts Wall

School visit photos, stories & "Mark as Done".

4. Queries Hub

Public doubt clearing across all batches.

5. Materials

Official Session form & Intern Toolkit links.

6. Reimbursements

Deadlines, claim logs & approval status.

7. Meet the Outreach Team

Strict hierarchy: Management → Senior Secretary → Junior Secretaries → Scholars.

8. Progress Intelligence Hub

Multi-dimensional filters (Month, Batch, Stream, Supervisor) & 1-Click Excel (.CSV) export.
